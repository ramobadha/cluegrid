      Rules & recommended setup

      Start with 4–10 players for an easy first game; 3–20 are supported. Use 1 imposter for 3–6 players, 2 for 7–11, or 3 for 12–20. The host can change this, keeping imposters below half the group.

      Different word: ordinary players share one word; imposters share a related word. Roles stay unknown until the final reveal. No word: imposters see that they have no word and must bluff.

      Go around the table giving clues, then discuss and vote. Resolve ties with another clue and vote. Eliminated players stop giving clues and voting. Suggested house rule: the group wins by eliminating all imposters; imposters win if their remaining number equals the ordinary players. Agree on optional last-chance word guesses before starting. The app does not track votes or choose a winner.
